# Metrics

## North Star Metric

**Audits completed per week**

This is the right North Star because every other outcome (leads captured, Credex consultations booked, revenue) flows from someone completing an audit. A user who completes an audit has seen value. DAU would be wrong here — this is a tool people use once a quarter, not daily.

## 3 Input Metrics

**1. Homepage to audit completion rate**
Measures what % of visitors who land on the homepage actually complete and submit the audit. Target: >40%. If this is low, the form is too long or confusing.

**2. Email capture rate**
Measures what % of users who complete an audit submit their email. Target: >25%. This is the lead generation metric — without emails, Credex can't follow up.

**3. Shareability rate**
Measures what % of users copy their shareable URL. Target: >15%. This drives organic growth — every shared link is a free acquisition channel.

## What I'd Instrument First
1. Audit completion rate (form submit → results page load)
2. Email capture rate (results page → email submitted)
3. Share button clicks per audit
4. Average monthly savings shown per audit
5. Credex CTA clicks (for >$500 savings audits)

## Pivot Trigger
If after 500 audits completed:
- Email capture rate is below 10% → redesign the email capture flow, test different value propositions
- Average savings shown is below $50 → audit engine rules need more aggressive optimization logic
- Credex CTA click rate is below 2% → Credex offer is not compelling enough, test different messaging
