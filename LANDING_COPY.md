## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login required. You get the full audit for free. We only ask for your email if you want the report emailed to you.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: All pricing is verified weekly against official vendor pricing pages. Every number has a source URL. We flag when prices change.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: We store your audit results (tools and savings) to generate your shareable URL. If you provide your email, we store that too. We never sell your data.

**Q: What AI tools do you support?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. More coming soon.

**Q: What happens after I submit my email?**
A: You get a confirmation email with your audit results. If your savings are significant, our team at Credex may reach out with options to save even more through discounted AI credits.
