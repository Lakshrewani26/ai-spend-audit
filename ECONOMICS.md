# Unit Economics

## What's a Converted Lead Worth to Credex?

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Assume:
- Average deal size: $500/month in AI credits purchased
- Gross margin: ~30% (discounted credits still have margin)
- Average customer lifetime: 12 months

**LTV per customer = $500 × 30% × 12 = $1,800**

Even a conservative $200/month deal at 30% margin for 6 months = $360 LTV. So a converted lead is worth roughly **$360-$1,800** depending on company size.

## CAC at Each Channel

| Channel | Est. CAC | Reasoning |
|---------|----------|-----------|

| Hacker News | $0 | Free post, organic traffic |
| Reddit | $0 | Free posts, organic traffic |
| Twitter/X | $0 | Free tweets, viral sharing |
| Cold DMs | $2-5 | Time cost only, ~20 DMs/hour |
| Paid ads (future) | $50-150 | Estimated B2B SaaS CAC |

At $0 CAC from organic channels and $1,800 LTV, the unit economics are extremely favorable in early stage.

## Conversion Funnel

| Stage | Conversion Rate | Numbers |
|-------|----------------|---------|

| Visitor → Audit completed | 35% | 1000 visitors → 350 audits |
| Audit → Email captured | 25% | 350 audits → 87 leads |
| Email → Consultation booked | 10% | 87 leads → 9 consultations |
| Consultation → Purchase | 30% | 9 consultations → 3 customers |

So roughly **3 customers per 1000 visitors** at $0 CAC.
At $1,800 LTV that's **$5,400 revenue per 1000 visitors.**

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

**What needs to be true:**

$1M ARR = ~$83,000/month recurring revenue
At $500/month average deal = 166 active customers needed

**Month 1-3: Seed the funnel**
- 500 audits/month from organic channels
- 125 leads/month
- 12 consultations/month
- 4 new customers/month
- MRR after 3 months: ~$2,000

**Month 4-9: Content + referral flywheel kicks in**
- Shared audit URLs drive organic traffic
- Target 2,000 audits/month
- 500 leads/month
- 50 consultations/month
- 15 new customers/month
- MRR after 9 months: ~$20,000

**Month 10-18: Paid acquisition + partnerships**
- Add paid ads at $50 CAC, still profitable vs $1,800 LTV
- Partner with startup accelerators (YC, Antler) for bulk audits
- Target 10,000 audits/month
- MRR after 18 months: ~$85,000 → $1M ARR

**Key assumption:** Credex closes 30% of consultations. If close rate drops to 10%, path to $1M ARR takes 36 months instead of 18.

## What Would Have to Be True

1. Organic distribution works — HN/Reddit drives consistent traffic
2. Audit engine shows meaningful savings for >60% of users
3. Credex sales team can handle consultation volume
4. Average deal size stays above $300/month
5. Churn stays below 5%/month (customers keep buying credits)
